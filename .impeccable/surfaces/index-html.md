## Scope

`index.html` (the rack) and `projects/<PART-NO>/index.html` (the coupon). Visitor
mode: **Experience** — the geometry leads from the first viewport; the interface
recedes to stamps and rules around it.

## Audience and job

A hiring manager or recruiter skimming a link between meetings, and a
prospective freelance client sizing up competence. Both want proof of modelling
ability before biography. A technically literate second reader arrives later and
inspects fillets, proportion, and assembly relationships closely.

## Action

Primary: contact Christopher (email, one click, from the title block or the
sticky nameplate). Secondary: open a coupon and rotate the geometry.

## Proof and content

Real geometry the visitor rotates — not renders of geometry. Every project
carries a spec stamp (part no., material, process, finish, Ra, mass, software)
and a revision table showing what changed and why, which is the evidence a
technical reader actually weighs.

Until Christopher supplies material, every project, credential, and contact
detail is authored placeholder, stamped `UNCONTROLLED COPY / NOT FOR ISSUE` at
page level so it cannot ship by accident.

## Constraints

Static files on GitHub Pages, no build step. Meshes are heavy and load on
intent behind a poster. Full content and contact path survive with JavaScript
off or WebGL absent.

## Chosen direction

**The Finish Sample Card** (seed 66124021, grounded candidate 6). The site is an
anodize dye-chip card: each project is a machined sample coupon on a bead-blast
field, spec stamped alongside, dye rack standing to the right.

Raises taken from declined worlds: designed absence (ghost coupons for unissued
slots); front sells / back specifies at full saturation; composed emptiness
around the hero; one fixed cell unit and part-number addressing; the artifact
records its own making (revision tables).

## Memorable moment

**The finish selector.** STL carries no material data, so the site art-directs
the finish — and hands that control to the visitor. Picking a dye chip
re-finishes the live model *and* retints the entire page accent, because the
page's accent colour is the selected finish. The format's limitation becomes
the signature interaction.
